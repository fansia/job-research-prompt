03 · What the report covers

    
Five parts, in clear bullet points

    

      
Company and industryWhere it stands
Business model, stage, news and controversies from the last one to two years (including leadership changes), and competitors.

      
What the job really isThe day to day
Daily tasks, how performance is measured, and the skills and traits that matter, with why.

      
Pay, hours and benefitsOnly where there's data
Salary range for similar roles, overtime and time off, benefits people like and the ones they complain about.

      
What people actually sayPros, pitfalls, biases
A synthesis of Taiwanese forums and review sites (Dcard, PTT, GoodJob), with a note on how those reviews can be biased.

      
Where the job leadsCareer path
Promotion and transfers inside the company, options outside it, the skills you'd build, and the risks.

    

  


  

    
04 · When information is thin

    
The prompt tells the AI: if there's little information or only scattered posts, say so ("limited data, individual experiences, not representative of the whole company") instead of filling the gap; and never judge from a single person's review.

    
Still check what the AI writes, and open the cited links yourself.

  


  

    
05 · FAQ

    
Questions

    
Does it cost anything? Do I need an account?
No and no. It's a prompt: copy it into whichever AI you use. Whether that AI is free is up to the platform.

    
Which AI works best?
One that searches the web, like Perplexity or Gemini, so the report cites its sources. Models without search only know what they were trained on, so news and pay can be out of date.

    
Does anyone collect the job link I type?
This is a static page with no backend. The link stays in your own browser for next time; when you press a button it goes, with the prompt, to the AI you picked.

    
Can I trust the pay and reviews in the report?
No. They are the AI's estimates from public data, and the prompt asks it to flag thin data. Check anything important yourself before an interview.
